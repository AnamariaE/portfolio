Wayuu Learning Circles

Wikimedia → WikiLearn → Wayuu Learning Circles · Project and narrative

The format adapts to the community, not the other way around.

The Wayuu Learning Circles and their narrative are part of WikiLearn, within my work with Wikimedia. The project began with cultural research and direct collaboration before any learning asset was designed.

Approach
Understand how knowledge is created, shared and lived in the community, then use that logic to shape the digital experience.
Deliverables
Narrative video, visual guide, low-text infographics, interactive HTML components and a facilitator learning journey.
Principle
Accessibility is not a concession or a final checklist. It is the standard that makes the work clearer for everyone.
