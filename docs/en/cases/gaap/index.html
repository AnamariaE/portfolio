THE PROJECT

Designing for the reality outside the brief.

GAAP serves rural communities where connectivity, literacy and familiarity with digital platforms cannot be assumed.

51 learning objects

Challenge
Translate complex microfinance and agricultural methodologies into learning that stays clear under real-world constraints.
My role
End-to-end instructional design, narrative architecture, character system, gamification and multimedia production methodology.
What changed
A nine-module world with 51 learning objects, three original characters and image- and audio-led experiences built for low bandwidth.
Outcomes
Over 85% participation · 4.5/5 rating.
