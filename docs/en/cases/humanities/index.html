THE KEY TO THIS CASE

THE PROJECT

A structure that continued beyond its creation.

As a lecturer at UCA, El Salvador, I created Introduction to Digital Humanities and gamified the entire course through Aprendiz a Crononauta in Moodle. It served 120 students across two editions in 2021. After I left UCA, the gamification system continued to be used in 2024.

120 students in 2021

UCA · TEACHING & ACADEMIC COORDINATION

Teaching, creating and coordinating.

From 2016 to 2022, I worked at Universidad Centroamericana José Simeón Cañas, El Salvador, as a lecturer and academic coordinator. My adjunct teaching in the master’s programme ran from 2021 to 2023.
